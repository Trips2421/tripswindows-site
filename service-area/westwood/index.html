The Westwood factor

Why Westwood homes need consistent exterior maintenance.

Westwood's density is the maintenance challenge. High-rise glass on the Wilshire Corridor accumulates marine moisture, pollution, and bird debris. Village storefronts collect fingerprints, food residue, and pedestrian smudges that residential buildings don't see. Single-family homes south of the Village experience the same Westside marine layer as Brentwood and Mar Vista.

High-rise residential needs specialized approach. The Wilshire Corridor's towers — from the Carlyle to the Wilshire Manning to all the rest — have exterior glass that requires rope-access or boom-lift work for full facade cleaning. Individual condo balcony glass and interior glass are handled by our standard crews. We coordinate with HOA managers and concierge teams.

Commercial demand cycles drive Village service. UCLA's academic calendar shifts foot traffic, retail volume, and service needs. Village restaurants and retail benefit from pre-quarter cleaning, game-day prep, and post-finals window touch-ups. We work around the campus rhythm.

What we recommend
  • Wilshire Corridor condos — quarterly interior and balcony glass. Full-facade exterior arranged with HOAs and rope-access partners.
  • Westwood Village storefronts — weekly to biweekly. Pre-dawn or post-close service keeps retail glass spotless.
  • Single-family homes south of Wilshire — quarterly. Standard Westside marine-layer maintenance.
  • UCLA-adjacent properties — quarterly. Student housing, fraternity/sorority houses, faculty homes.
  • Office building lobbies and exteriors — weekly to biweekly. Westwood's office density matches the Village's retail.
  • Pressure washing — twice yearly minimum for sidewalks, patios, and entry plazas. Heavy pedestrian traffic embeds grime.
