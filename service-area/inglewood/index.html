Inglewood sits inland from the coast compared to beachfront communities, but still experiences measurable salt spray and coastal wind patterns. Newer areas near SoFi Stadium benefit from modern architectural designs with glass — contemporary homes and mixed-use development need clean, clear glass to showcase their design.

Commercial properties throughout Inglewood — especially around The Forum and in downtown's revitalizing corridors — demand professional presentation.
