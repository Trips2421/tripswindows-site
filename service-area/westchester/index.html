The Westchester challenge

Why Westchester homes need specialized cleaning.

Westchester's unique geography creates a triple threat for window maintenance: coastal salt air, marine layer moisture, and jet exhaust particles from LAX. Homes directly under the flight path accumulate a fine particulate film that regular water-and-squeegee cleaning cannot dissolve.

Jet exhaust leaves behind carbon, sulfur compounds, and metal oxides that bond to glass. Over time, this buildup becomes micropitted — actually scratching the glass surface. Standard residential window cleaning misses this because it focuses on mineral film removal. Westchester homes need purified water systems and scrubbing techniques specifically designed to lift particulate matter before it etches.

Even homes slightly away from the direct flight path still benefit from aggressive coastal cleaning protocols. The combination of LAX proximity and ocean moisture means Westchester windows deteriorate faster than inland neighborhoods. Quarterly is the minimum for most homes; bimonthly is better for flight-path properties or south-facing glass.

What we recommend
  • Direct flight-path homes — monthly or bimonthly window cleaning. Jet exhaust accumulation is aggressive and needs specialist purified water treatment.
  • Kentwood & mid-century homes — quarterly. Aluminum frame windows collect mineral film faster; quarterly keeps them bright and prevents corrosion.
  • Newer construction with modern glass — quarterly baseline, bimonthly if heavily south- or west-facing. Modern windows show every speck.
  • Loyola Village & quieter areas — quarterly standard. Less direct flight exposure but still coastal exposure.
  • Pressure washing — twice yearly for driveways and patios. Marine moisture promotes algae and mildew growth year-round.
  • Gutter cleaning — biannual. Coastal salt and jet exhaust accelerate roof deterioration; clean gutters prevent water damage from mineral-contaminated runoff.
