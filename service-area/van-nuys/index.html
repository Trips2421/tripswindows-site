The Van Nuys factor

Why Van Nuys properties need regular professional cleaning.

Van Nuys sits in the geographic heart of the San Fernando Valley — one of Los Angeles' hottest, dustiest microclimates. Summer temperatures exceed 95°F regularly, baking dust and grime onto glass faster than homeowners can keep up. The heat doesn't just make windows dirty — it bonds particulate to the surface, making it harder to remove the longer it sits. Hard water deposits are particularly pronounced in this part of the Valley.

Van Nuys Boulevard's commercial density means retail and office properties face serious grime challenges — foot traffic, vehicle exhaust, and retail activity combine to create a particular kind of soiling that's different from residential neighborhoods. Storefronts show every fingerprint and dust speck. The Civic Center's government buildings require a professional, reliable service schedule. Tree coverage near Lake Balboa and the Sepulveda Basin generates constant leaf drop and organic debris.

Multi-unit buildings have particularly large glass surfaces that show dirt across wide areas. Dust is relentless year-round, and Santa Ana winds in fall and winter deposit additional fine sediment. For commercial properties especially, regular professional cleaning is the only way to maintain the pristine appearance necessary to compete for customer and business attention.

What we recommend
  • Most Van Nuys residential homes — quarterly service. Valley heat and dust make quarterly the baseline.
  • Homes near Lake Balboa & Basin — every 2–3 months. Tree coverage and debris are constant sources of glass soiling.
  • Van Nuys Boulevard commercial properties — weekly to biweekly. Visibility and foot traffic make frequent cleaning essential.
  • Civic Center & government buildings — weekly to biweekly. Professional municipal service requires reliability and consistency.
  • Multi-unit buildings & apartments — biweekly to monthly exterior glass. Large glass surfaces and common areas need regular attention.
  • Pressure washing — 2–3 times per year for residential, quarterly for commercial. Valley heat and dust stain hardscape quickly.
  • Gutter cleaning — twice a year minimum. Tree coverage and debris accumulation are common throughout Van Nuys.
  • Auto dealerships & retail — weekly service. Visibility is everything in competitive commercial environments.
