Why Eagle Rock windows need consistent care.

It's the combination of trees, college activity, and commercial traffic. The residential neighborhoods have mature oak and pine canopy that drops sap, pollen, and leaf debris year-round. The Occidental College area experiences campus grounds maintenance dust and seasonal construction from building improvements. Colorado Boulevard carries commercial traffic and foot traffic that generates dust and urban grime.

The Craftsman homes that give Eagle Rock character have period windows that show dirt visibly and require careful handling. The college-town demographic means student rentals and higher property turnover in some areas — these homes need regular maintenance to stay presentable. And the family-focused culture means residents care about how their homes look and function — clean windows are part of that standard.

Eagle Rock is stable and rooted. Residents take care of their properties. Regular quarterly window cleaning is the expectation, especially for homes with views and visible windows that define the home's curb appeal.

What we recommend
  • Craftsman homes — quarterly with care for period details. Original windows show grime and require attentive maintenance.
  • Midcentury homes — quarterly minimum. Larger window areas and clean design lines mean windows define the home's character.
  • Hill Drive & elevated areas — quarterly or bimonthly. Tree canopy and heat trapping mean pollen and sap accumulation.
  • College-adjacent residential — quarterly or bimonthly depending on construction phases. Campus maintenance and building projects create dust.
  • Colorado Boulevard commercial — weekly or biweekly. Storefronts and retail need consistent curb appeal.
  • Pressure washing — seasonally or annually. Dust and commercial corridor grime build up on patios, driveways, walkways.
  • Gutter cleaning — twice yearly. Oak and pine debris clogs fast in Eagle Rock's tree-covered neighborhoods.
