Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. Here's how we collect, use, and protect your information.

Information We Collect

When you request a free estimate, contact us, or interact with our website, we may collect information including your name, email address, phone number, and home address. We collect this information solely to respond to your inquiry and provide you with service details. Additionally, we gather basic analytics data about how you use our website, such as the pages you visit and how long you spend on each page, through Google Analytics.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to respond to estimate requests, schedule services, send appointment confirmations, and answer your questions. We analyze website usage data to understand how visitors interact with our site, which helps us improve our services and the user experience. We never sell, share, or distribute your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics (GA4) to understand how people use our website. Google Analytics collects anonymized data about your visit, including which pages you view, how long you stay, and where you came from. This data is aggregated and does not personally identify you. If you prefer not to be tracked by Google Analytics, you can install Google's opt-out browser add-on, which prevents Analytics from collecting your data.

Cookies

Our website uses minimal cookies for analytics purposes only. These cookies help us understand user behavior and improve the website. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time, though this may affect some website functionality.

Third-Party Services

We use Cloudflare for website hosting and Google Analytics for site analytics. Each of these services maintains their own privacy practices and policies. We encourage you to review their privacy policies to understand how they handle data.

Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, update, or delete your personal information at any time. If you wish to see what information we have collected about you, or if you'd like us to delete your information from our records, please contact us using the information provided below.
