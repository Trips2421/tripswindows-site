What to Expect During Professional Window Cleaning

April 29, 2026 · 3 min read

Scheduling your first professional window cleaning appointment can feel overwhelming if you're not sure what to expect. Will they rearrange your furniture? How long will it take? What should you prepare? At Trip's Windows, we've cleaned thousands of Los Angeles-area homes, and we want to walk you through every step of the process so you feel confident and prepared.

Step 1: Booking Your Appointment

Getting started is simple. Give us a call or text to schedule a free estimate. We'll discuss your home's size, number of windows, and any special concerns (hard water stains, high-rise cleaning, screen repairs). Our team will provide a no-pressure quote and find a time that works for your schedule. We serve West LA, Santa Monica, Venice, Brentwood, Pacific Palisades, Beverly Hills, Culver City, and Manhattan Beach, plus parts of Orange County.

Step 2: The Day of Your Cleaning

Expect a uniformed crew to arrive in our branded van during your scheduled window. We'll knock on the door, introduce ourselves, and do a quick walkthrough of your home to assess the scope. We'll check for any damaged frames, problem areas like hard water deposits, or windows that need special attention. This is your chance to point out anything you'd like us to focus on.

Step 3: The Cleaning Process

Once we start, you'll see professional equipment in action. For interior and lower-level windows, we use high-quality squeegees and professional cleaning solutions. For upper floors and high windows, we use water-fed poles—extending equipment that lets us reach safely without ladders. We use purified water to avoid streaks and water spots, a game-changer for crystal-clear results. Every window frame and sill gets wiped down, not just the glass. The whole process is efficient and non-disruptive—we won't need to rearrange your furniture or move personal items.

Step 4: Post-Clean Walkthrough

Before we leave, we'll walk through with you one more time. We point out what we cleaned, answer any questions, and make sure you're completely satisfied with the results. This is also when we discuss payment and explain our 48-hour re-clean guarantee—if you spot any streaks or missed spots within two days, we'll come back and make it right at no extra cost.

Why Professional Cleaning Matters

Professional window cleaning isn't just about sparkling glass—it protects your investment. We spot potential issues like damaged seals, cracked frames, or debris buildup that can lead to bigger problems down the road. For Los Angeles homeowners in sunny areas like Venice and Santa Monica, regular cleaning also helps your windows last longer by removing salt air, dust, and mineral deposits.
