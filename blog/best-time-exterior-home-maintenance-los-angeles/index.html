The Best Time of Year for Exterior Home Maintenance in Los Angeles

May 11, 2026 · 4 min read

The best time for exterior home maintenance in Los Angeles is spring (March through May) and fall (September through November). LA's mild, Mediterranean climate makes year-round work possible, but each service has an ideal window. Scheduling window cleaning, gutter cleaning, and pressure washing at the right time of year means better results, less rework, and a home that stays protected through every season.

Spring: The Post-Rain Reset (March–May)

Spring is when Los Angeles shakes off the rainy season. By March, the last significant storms have usually passed, and it's the perfect time to assess and restore your home's exterior. Start with gutters—months of rain have likely pushed leaves, grit, and rooftop debris into your gutter system. A thorough gutter cleaning after the final rains ensures everything drains properly heading into the dry months.

Pressure washing is especially effective in spring. Winter rain leaves behind dirt, algae, and mineral deposits on driveways, walkways, and siding. A professional pressure wash strips away that buildup while surfaces are still damp enough to release grime easily. For windows, late April and May are ideal—after pollen season peaks. Cleaning windows too early means a fresh coat of pollen within days. Waiting until the bloom settles gives you crystal-clear glass that actually lasts.

Summer: Open House Season (June–August)

Summer in LA means entertaining. Backyard gatherings, Fourth of July parties, and long evenings with the doors wide open. This is the season when your home's appearance matters most to you and your guests. Schedule window cleaning in early June to maximize visibility and natural light for the months ahead. Clean windows make a noticeable difference in how bright and open your home feels, especially in neighborhoods like Venice and Santa Monica where ocean light is a defining feature.

Summer is also the right time to inspect and repair window and door screens. With indoor-outdoor living at its peak, torn or ill-fitting screens invite flies, mosquitoes, and dust. A quick screen check and repair keeps your home comfortable without sacrificing fresh air. If you're getting your home summer-ready, clean screens and sparkling windows should be at the top of the list.

Fall: Pre-Rain Prep (September–November)

Fall is the most critical season for exterior home maintenance in Southern California. The dry months have left dust and debris everywhere, and November's rains are approaching fast. This is the window where preventive work pays off the most.

Gutter cleaning in October or early November is essential. Clogged gutters during LA's first heavy rain cause overflow, foundation pooling, and landscape erosion—damage that's entirely preventable with a single cleaning. If you're wondering how often to clean gutters in Southern California, fall is the one appointment you should never skip.

Pressure washing before the holiday season refreshes your home's curb appeal for Thanksgiving and December gatherings. Driveways, patios, and entryways accumulate months of dust, tire marks, and foot traffic during the dry season. A thorough wash in October transforms the look of your property. Pair it with window cleaning, and your home is guest-ready from the street to the living room.

Winter: The Rainy Season Reality (December–February)

Winter is LA's quietest season for exterior maintenance, and for good reason. Rain makes outdoor cleaning less practical—pressure washing between storms is inefficient, and freshly cleaned windows won't stay clean for long. But winter isn't a total maintenance blackout.

Interior window cleaning is perfectly suited to the rainy months. Dust, cooking residue, and condensation build up on interior glass year-round, and winter is an ideal time to address it without worrying about exterior conditions. After major storms, a quick gutter check is also smart. Heavy rain can shift debris, dislodge downspout connections, or reveal damage that wasn't visible before. Catching these issues between storms prevents compounding problems through the rest of the rainy season.

A Simple Annual Maintenance Calendar

Keeping track of seasonal tasks doesn't need to be complicated. Here's a straightforward breakdown of when to schedule each service throughout the year.

March through May: Clean gutters after the last rains. Pressure wash driveways, walkways, and siding to remove winter buildup. Schedule window cleaning in late April or May after pollen subsides.

June through August: Clean windows before summer entertaining. Inspect and repair screens for indoor-outdoor living season. Spot-clean high-traffic exterior surfaces as needed.

September through November: Clean gutters before the first November rains—this is the most important appointment of the year. Pressure wash patios and entryways before holiday gatherings. Clean windows inside and out for maximum natural light heading into shorter days.

December through February: Focus on interior window cleaning. Perform post-storm gutter inspections after heavy rain events. Plan and schedule your spring maintenance ahead of time.

Why Bundling Services Saves Time

One of the smartest moves for LA homeowners is bundling exterior maintenance into a single visit. When the same crew handles window cleaning, gutter cleaning, and pressure washing on the same day, you avoid the hassle of coordinating multiple appointments, being home for several different service visits, and having work spread across weeks. It also means one crew with one setup, working efficiently across your entire property instead of repeating mobilization for each individual task.

Bundling is especially practical in fall, when gutters, windows, and hardscapes all need attention before the rains arrive. Instead of booking three separate services across October and November, a single combined visit handles everything at once. Your home goes from dusty and rain-vulnerable to fully prepped in a single morning.
