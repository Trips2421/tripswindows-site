How to Prepare for a Window Cleaning Appointment: A Homeowner's Checklist

2026-06-25 · 7 min read

Booking a professional window cleaning is easy. Getting your home ready for it takes about ten minutes — and that ten minutes is the difference between a crew that breezes through and one that spends half its time working around your stuff. Here's exactly what to do before the appointment, what you can skip, and what to expect when the crew arrives.

What do I need to do before a window cleaning appointment?

Before a window cleaning appointment, do five quick things: clear window sills and ledges of decor and valuables, move large furniture a few feet back from interior windows, clear exterior access by moving cars and unlocking gates, secure pets in a closed room, and make sure the crew can get in.

None of it takes long, and it lets the crew spend their time cleaning glass rather than working around obstacles — which means a faster visit and better results. The rest of this guide walks through each step and answers the questions homeowners ask most. If this is your first time, you may also want to read what to expect during a professional window cleaning.

Should I move furniture before window cleaners arrive?

Yes, move large furniture a few feet away from any window the crew will clean from the inside — beds, dressers, sofas, and desks pushed up against glass. You don't need to empty the room; just create a clear path so cleaners can reach the window safely without leaning over your furniture.

Most crews will help nudge heavier pieces, but having it done in advance speeds the visit and reduces any risk to your belongings. The same goes for anything hanging in the window's path — pull back long curtains or tie up blinds so the glass is fully exposed.

Do I need to remove window screens before cleaning?

No — you don't need to remove window screens yourself. Professional window cleaners remove, clean, and reinstall screens as part of the service. In fact, removing them yourself can lead to bent frames or torn mesh if they're stuck or fragile.

Just leave them in place and let the crew handle them. If any screens are already damaged, mention it so they can flag whether repair or replacement makes sense — that's a quick add-on through our screen repair service.

Do I need to be home during window cleaning?

You don't have to be home the entire time, but the crew needs a way to access the interior windows and any locked exterior areas. Many homeowners let the crew in, point out priorities, and then go about their day.

If you can't be there, arrange access in advance — share a gate code, leave a key, or have someone available to let them in. For exterior-only cleanings, you often don't need to be home at all as long as gates are unlocked and the crew knows the plan.

What should I do with my pets during a window cleaning?

Secure pets in a closed room, crate, or backyard area away from the work. Window cleaning involves opening windows and doors, moving between inside and outside, and propping screens — all of which create chances for a pet to slip out or get underfoot near ladders.

Keeping them contained keeps them safe and lets the crew move efficiently. Let the company know in advance if you have pets so they can be careful with gates and doors, especially during exterior work.

Should I clean my windows before the window cleaners come?

No, there's no need to clean your windows before a professional cleaning — that's the whole point of the service. The crew handles dirt, dust, fingerprints, and grime as part of the job. The only useful prep is clearing items off sills and moving furniture so they can reach the glass.

If anything unusual is on the glass — paint overspray, stickers, or cloudy hard water staining — point it out so the crew can plan for it, since those often need special treatment beyond a standard clean.

How do I prepare the outside of my house for window cleaning?

To prepare the exterior, move vehicles out of the driveway and away from windows, unlock side gates, coil and move garden hoses out of walkways, and trim back any shrubs or branches blocking ground-floor windows. If you have a security system or dogs in the yard, disarm or secure them.

Clear, safe access to every exterior window lets the crew set ladders and use water-fed poles without delays — and protects your landscaping. Access matters even more for hillside and canyon properties, where reaching the glass is already the hard part, as we cover in our hillside home maintenance guide.

How long does a professional window cleaning take?

Most residential window cleanings take anywhere from one to four hours depending on the number of windows, the size of the home, whether it's interior-and-exterior or exterior-only, and access difficulty. A small single-story home with a dozen windows might take an hour or two; a large multi-story home with screens, skylights, and tricky access can take most of a day.

Doing the simple prep in advance keeps the visit on the shorter end — and if you're booking multiple services like windows plus gutters or pressure washing, expect to set aside more of the day for the full sequence.

Will window cleaners move my things or should I do it myself?

It's best to move small valuables, breakables, and personal items off sills and ledges yourself before the appointment. Reputable crews are careful, but they'd rather you handle anything fragile or sentimental so there's no question of responsibility.

For larger furniture, most crews will help shift pieces a short distance, but having heavy items pre-moved a few feet from the glass is appreciated and speeds things up. Think of it as the same courtesy you'd extend to a house painter or a carpet cleaner.

What if some of my windows are painted shut or hard to open?

Point out any windows that are painted shut, stuck, or have broken hardware before the crew starts. They won't force a window that could break or damage the frame. Knowing in advance lets them clean those windows from whichever side is accessible and avoids surprises.

The same goes for windows with delicate tint, leaded glass, or special coatings — flag them so the crew uses the right approach. Older and historic homes in particular benefit from this heads-up, since their glass and frames need a gentler hand.

What happens after the window cleaning is finished?

After cleaning, the crew typically does a walkthrough with you to check the glass in different light, reinstalls screens, and makes sure sills and tracks are wiped down. This is your chance to point out anything you'd like touched up.
