Should You Clean Windows Before Selling Your Home?

May 11, 2026 · 4 min read

Yes—professional window cleaning is one of the highest-impact, lowest-effort improvements you can make before listing your home. Clean windows transform how a property photographs, how it feels during showings, and how buyers perceive its overall condition. Real estate agents across Los Angeles consistently recommend it as part of pre-sale preparation because the difference is immediately visible and the effort required from the homeowner is minimal.

First Impressions Start With Natural Light

Buyers decide how they feel about a home within seconds of walking through the door. One of the biggest factors in that first impression is natural light. Dirty windows filter and dim sunlight, making rooms feel smaller, darker, and less inviting. Clean glass lets the full spectrum of light into every room, which makes spaces feel open, bright, and well-maintained.

This matters even more in listing photography. Real estate photographers rely on natural light to make interiors look their best. Smudged or hazy windows create dull reflections and uneven lighting in photos—the very photos that determine whether a buyer clicks through to schedule a showing. In a market like Los Angeles where homes often feature large windows, glass doors, and indoor-outdoor living spaces, the condition of your glass is front and center in almost every shot.

What Real Estate Agents in LA Recommend

Talk to any experienced listing agent in Los Angeles and window cleaning will be on their pre-sale checklist. The standard recommendation is to schedule professional window cleaning at three key moments during the selling process:

  • Before listing photos: Photography happens once and those images represent your home for the entire time it's on the market. Clean windows ensure every room photographs at its brightest and most appealing.
  • Before the first open house: Buyers at open houses notice details. Fingerprints on glass, water spots on exterior panes, and dusty screens all signal deferred maintenance—even if the rest of the home is in excellent condition.
  • Before final showings or inspections: As you approach closing, maintaining that clean presentation reinforces the buyer's confidence in their decision. A home that looks well cared for at every stage of the process gives buyers fewer reasons to negotiate.

Interior and Exterior Both Matter

It's tempting to focus only on the interior since that's what buyers see during showings. But exterior window cleaning is equally important. Buyers view the home from the curb, from the yard, and from neighboring properties. Dirty exterior glass is visible from a distance and undermines the curb appeal of even the most well-maintained landscaping.

There's also a practical issue: you can't get truly clean-looking windows by only cleaning one side. Interior grime reduces the light coming through, while exterior buildup creates haze and water spots that are visible from inside. Both sides need attention for the full effect. We break down the differences and why both matter in our guide to interior vs. exterior window cleaning.

Hard Water Stains Can Kill a Deal

Hard water stains are one of the most common window issues in Los Angeles. Sprinkler systems that spray onto glass leave mineral deposits that build up over time, creating cloudy white spots that won't come off with regular cleaning. These stains are especially common on ground-floor windows and glass patio doors.

During showings, hard water stains stand out. They make glass look permanently damaged, and buyers may assume the windows need full replacement—which can become a negotiating point or even a deal-breaker on higher-end properties. The good news is that professional hard water stain removal can restore glass to like-new condition in most cases. We cover the full process in our post on how to clean hard water stains off glass doors.

If you know your sprinklers have been hitting your windows, address the stains before listing. It's a much smaller project than most homeowners expect, and it eliminates a visible flaw that buyers will absolutely notice.

Pair It With Pressure Washing for Maximum Curb Appeal

Window cleaning delivers the best results when it's part of a full exterior refresh. Combining professional window cleaning with pressure washing your driveway, walkways, patio, and siding creates a comprehensive transformation that makes the entire property look cared for from every angle.

Pressure washing removes the accumulated grime, algae, and discoloration that builds up on hardscaping and exterior surfaces over time. Paired with sparkling windows, it creates a cohesive impression of a home that's been maintained with attention to detail. That's exactly the message you want to send to prospective buyers.

Many of our clients preparing to sell schedule both services on the same day. It's a single appointment that addresses curb appeal from the glass to the ground.
