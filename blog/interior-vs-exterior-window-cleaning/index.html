Interior vs Exterior Window Cleaning: Do You Need Both?

May 4, 2026 · 3 min read

When homeowners schedule window cleaning, one of the most common questions is whether they need both interior and exterior service. The short answer: for the best results, yes. But the reasons might surprise you.

What Exterior Window Cleaning Covers

Exterior glass faces everything—rain, dust, pollen, bird droppings, hard water from sprinklers, and in coastal neighborhoods like Venice and Santa Monica, constant salt spray and marine haze. Exterior cleaning removes all of this using purified water systems and professional squeegee techniques. It's also where most visible grime builds up, so the difference is dramatic. When you look out your window and see a haze or spots, that's almost always the exterior catching everything the environment throws at it.

What Interior Window Cleaning Covers

Interior glass collects fingerprints, cooking grease, pet nose prints, dust, and off-gassing residue from furniture and paint. It's subtler than exterior grime but becomes obvious when sunlight hits the glass at certain angles. Interior cleaning also covers the tracks, sills, and frames—areas where dust and debris accumulate and can affect how well your windows seal and operate. A clean sill and track mean your windows move smoothly and your home stays better insulated.

Why One Without the Other Doesn't Cut It

Cleaning only the exterior leaves interior grime that dulls the overall clarity. Cleaning only the interior means you'll still see dirt, water spots, and mineral deposits from outside. You've experienced this—you clean the inside of your windshield and it looks great until you notice the outside is filthy. Same principle. For truly clear glass, both sides need attention. Professional clarity means seeing through your windows with perfect transparency from every angle.

How Often Should You Do Each?

Most LA homes benefit from full interior + exterior cleaning twice a year. Coastal properties (Venice, Manhattan Beach, Santa Monica) should do quarterly exterior at minimum, with interior matching or every other visit. Homes with pets, kids, or smokers benefit from more frequent interior service. Our crew handles both in a single visit—no need to schedule separately. We plan our schedule to make maintaining clear windows easy and convenient for you.

What to Expect When You Book Both

When you book both interior and exterior service, here's how it works: our crew arrives and does a brief walkthrough to assess your home. We start with the exterior first (usually with water-fed poles or ladders depending on height), then move inside to handle the glass, tracks, sills, and frames. Most homes take 2–4 hours depending on size and window count. Everything is done in one visit with the same crew, so you're only disrupted once. Protect Your Home and Keep It Looking Its Best

Whether you go with both services or start with exterior only, regular cleaning protects your glass and keeps your home looking its best.
