5 Signs Your Gutters Need Cleaning Before It's Too Late

May 4, 2026 · 3 min read

Your gutters work quietly until they don't. Most homeowners don't think about them until there's a problem—but by then, the damage is already happening. Here are the warning signs to watch for before gutter maintenance becomes an urgent problem.

Water Spilling Over the Edges

Overflowing gutters during rain means blockage. Water cascading down your siding causes staining, wood rot, and foundation damage over time. In Los Angeles, this matters most during the November–March rainy season when storms are intense but infrequent, making them easy to overlook until real damage sets in. If you're seeing water pouring over the sides instead of through the downspouts, your gutters are telling you they need attention.

Sagging or Pulling Away From the Fascia

Gutters that sag or pull away from your roofline are weighed down by debris, standing water, or both. This structural strain can damage your fascia board and eventually pull roofing material loose, compromising your home's weatherproofing. Hillside homes in Pacific Palisades and Brentwood are especially prone to this from heavy leaf and branch debris that accumulates faster than homeowners realize. If your gutters look like they're taking a bow, professional gutter cleaning in Pacific Palisades or your area can prevent serious structural damage.

Plants Growing in Your Gutters

If you see green sprouting from your gutters, there's enough accumulated organic matter to support plant growth. This means water is pooling and sediment has built up—a sign cleaning is long overdue. Plants rooting in your gutters also crack and damage the material itself, creating leaks that worsen with every rainfall. What looks like a minor detail is actually a sign that moisture and debris have been sitting there far longer than they should.

Pests and Birds Nesting

Clogged gutters become habitat for mosquitoes (standing water provides breeding grounds), birds, squirrels, and rodents. If you're noticing increased pest activity near your roofline—or worse, seeing nests in your gutters—dirty gutters could be the cause. The standing water and decomposing leaves create the perfect environment for insects and animals to settle in, and once they establish nesting sites, removal becomes complicated and expensive.

Staining on Your Siding or Foundation

Water marks, mineral streaks, or mold patches on your exterior walls directly below the gutter line are telltale signs of overflow that's been happening for a while. This staining isn't just cosmetic—it indicates water damage is happening behind the scenes. Mold growth in particular signals moisture penetration that threatens your home's structural integrity and air quality.

When to Schedule Gutter Cleaning

Don't wait for the signs above to appear. Preventive cleaning protects your investment far better than reactive repairs. We recommend twice a year minimum for most Los Angeles homes—once before rainy season (October/November) and once after spring. Homes near mature trees like those in Pasadena and Sherman Oaks should schedule cleaning three times per year to manage the heavy leaf fall.
