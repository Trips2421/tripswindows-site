Calabasas cleaning schedule

How often should you clean windows in Calabasas?

Frequency depends on oak canopy exposure and proximity to mountain roads.

Standard Calabasas homes

Quarterly service (winter, spring, summer, fall). Oak pollen, mountain dust, and Santa Ana wind deposits accumulate steadily in the foothill communities. Four cleanings per year maintains clear, sharp glass.

Large estate properties

Quarterly to bi-monthly for homes with extensive glass — oversized picture windows, multi-story facades, and glass-walled living areas. When your home's architecture centers on natural light and mountain views, hazy glass undermines the design.

Mountain-adjacent homes

Bi-monthly or as needed for properties along Mulholland Corridor and the upper hillside areas. Proximity to unpaved mountain roads and open space means heavier dust deposits, especially during dry months and wind events.

Post-Santa Ana wind service

As needed after major wind events. Santa Ana conditions drive fine desert particulate across the entire San Fernando Valley side of the mountains, coating Calabasas homes with a gritty residue that demands prompt cleaning.
