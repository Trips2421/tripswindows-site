Why gutter cleaning matters in Santa Monica.

Santa Monica's coastal environment creates unique gutter challenges. Salt spray from the Pacific deposits mineral residue on gutters constantly — faster than gutters in inland neighborhoods accumulate debris. This salt accelerates corrosion of aluminum and steel gutters, and when combined with clogged debris, prevents water from flowing freely. Eucalyptus trees — planted throughout Santa Monica's residential neighborhoods and commercial corridors — shed fine leaves and oil-rich needles year-round, adding to debris accumulation.

When gutters clog in Santa Monica, two problems compound: water backs up along the roofline and exterior walls (causing damage and staining), and salt-laden water sits longer in the gutter system, accelerating rust and deterioration. A clogged gutter in Santa Monica doesn't just overflow — it corrodes.

Trip's crew understands coastal home maintenance. We clear every gutter by hand, flush every downspout, and sweep roof edges. We inspect for salt-related corrosion and can flag areas where gutter repair or replacement may be needed soon.
