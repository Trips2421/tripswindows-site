Why Santa Monica properties need regular pressure washing.

Santa Monica sits directly on the Pacific with the Santa Monica Pier attracting ocean spray across the entire municipality. Mineral-laden salt mist settles on every outdoor surface daily — driveways, patios, walkways, and pool decks all develop hard white stains that household cleaning cannot touch.

The marine layer and year-round fog create persistent moisture. Homes near the Pier or in Ocean Park face heavier exposure than those in the higher elevations near Sunset. Properties with heavy tree cover (like along San Vicente Boulevard) trap moisture and develop algae blooms and mildew. Pool decks become especially slippery from green algae growth.

Santa Monica homeowners benefit from pressure washing 2–3 times per year — spring to remove winter mineral buildup, and late summer before entertaining season. Many residents schedule service quarterly for pristine year-round appearance.
