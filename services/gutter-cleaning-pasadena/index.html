Why gutter cleaning matters in Pasadena.

Pasadena's identity is rooted in its Craftsman homes and heritage neighborhoods. Many of these homes feature original or period-appropriate gutters — materials and profiles that differ from modern systems. The neighborhoods are equally defined by mature oak trees, many planted alongside homes when they were built decades ago. These oaks shed constantly, especially acorns and spring growth.

Gutter maintenance in Pasadena requires understanding period construction. Original gutters were often installed with different fastening systems, materials, and slope tolerances than contemporary gutters. When we clean your gutters, we respect these details. We understand the difference between the wide, shallow gutters typical of 1920s Craftsman homes and modern K-gutters. We won't damage period hardware or fasteners during cleaning.

Trip's crew approaches Pasadena homes with restoration-minded care. We clear gutters thoroughly, preserve period details, inspect for wear appropriate to your home's age, and treat your property as the heritage home it is.
