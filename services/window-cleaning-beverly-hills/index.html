Beverly Hills cleaning schedule

How often should you clean windows in Beverly Hills?

Frequency depends on landscape watering and canyon exposure.

Standard hillside homes

Twice yearly (spring after heavy winter rains, and fall before the holidays). Standard schedule for most Beverly Hills properties.

Canyon and multi-story estates

Quarterly or three times yearly if homes are heavily shaded by landscaping or receive significant debris from mature trees.

High-irrigation properties

Three times yearly if your landscape irrigation system regularly sprays windows and creates hard water buildup. Spring, summer, and fall recommended.

Commercial retail (Rodeo Drive area)

Weekly to monthly for boutiques and service businesses on Rodeo Drive. Clean windows essential for retail competitive advantage.
