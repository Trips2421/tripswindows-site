Coastal window care

Window cleaning in Venice requires a different approach than inland LA.

Venice sits right on the coast, which means your windows face constant salt spray and marine haze. The white, crusty mineral deposits that build up on glass, frames, and fixtures are salt residue — not regular dirt. This buildup doesn't respond well to standard cleaning methods and accelerates glass corrosion if left untreated.

Our crews are based here and know exactly what Venice properties face. We recommend quarterly service for homes within a quarter-mile of the coast, and every four to six weeks for canal-adjacent properties. Abbot Kinney storefronts and high-traffic commercial spaces need biweekly or weekly cleaning to maintain curb appeal. Regular maintenance extends the life of your windows and prevents expensive replacement down the road.

What's included

Exterior glass

Interior glass

Venice cleaning schedule

How often should you clean windows in Venice?

Coastal location means more frequent service than inland neighborhoods.

Canal-adjacent homes

Every 4 to 6 weeks for properties directly on or immediately next to Venice canals. Salt spray concentration is highest here.

Coastal Venice (within 1/4 mile of beach)

Quarterly (every 3 months) for homes near the coast, including Abbot Kinney area and Ocean Park neighborhoods.

Inland Venice

Twice yearly (spring and fall) for properties further inland from the coast but still within Venice proper.

Commercial storefronts

Weekly or biweekly for retail on Abbot Kinney, Main Street, and Ocean Park Boulevard to maintain storefront appeal year-round.
