Los Feliz pressure washing schedule

How often should you pressure wash in Los Feliz?

Frequency depends on surface type and Griffith Park exposure.

Driveways & concrete

Annually for most Los Feliz driveways. Steep grades mean water and debris flow concentrates in specific areas, creating heavy staining patterns. Semi-annual for driveways under heavy tree canopy.

Patios & tile surfaces

Annually or semi-annually depending on shade and exposure. Spanish tile patios beneath mature oaks and sycamores stain faster and may need attention twice per year.

Retaining walls & hardscape

Every one to two years for structural retaining walls. North-facing walls that stay damp develop algae faster and benefit from annual cleaning to prevent permanent discoloration.

Full property exterior

Annual comprehensive cleaning covers all hardscapes, stucco, and exterior surfaces in a single visit. Most effective when scheduled after fire season to remove accumulated ash and particulate.
