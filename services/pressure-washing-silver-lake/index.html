Silver Lake pressure washing schedule

How often should you pressure wash in Silver Lake?

Frequency depends on tree canopy, slope exposure, and shade coverage.

Typical Silver Lake homes

Two to three times yearly for most properties. Spring after winter rains flush hillside debris downslope, summer before outdoor entertaining season, and fall before the wet season returns.

Heavy canopy & shaded lots

Quarterly for properties under dense jacaranda, eucalyptus, or oak canopy. Shaded hardscapes develop algae and organic staining significantly faster than sun-exposed surfaces.

Steep driveways & staircase streets

Two to four times yearly depending on grade and sun exposure. Steep surfaces collect debris in textured concrete and become slippery hazards faster than flat surfaces after rain events.

Pre-event or pre-listing

One-time deep clean before open houses, parties, or seasonal entertaining. A professional pressure wash transforms Silver Lake curb appeal and makes outdoor spaces feel brand new.
