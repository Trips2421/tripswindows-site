Craftsman and hard water care

Pasadena's hard water is legendary—and your windows show it.

Pasadena has some of Southern California's hardest water. When irrigation systems spray water on windows, the mineral-rich spray dries leaving white, chalky deposits that regular cleaning can't remove. Combined with tree-lined neighborhoods that shed pollen and sap, Pasadena windows accumulate stubborn residue that requires specialized treatment.

We recommend spring and fall service for most Pasadena homes: spring catches pollen and early tree debris, fall prepares before the rainy season. Properties with heavy landscape irrigation may benefit from three-times-yearly service. Our crews have extensive experience removing hard water deposits using techniques specifically designed for Pasadena's mineral-heavy water situation.
