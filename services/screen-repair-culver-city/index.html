What we do

Every kind of screen repair you might need in Culver City.

Most screen problems fall into one of a handful of buckets — torn or sagging mesh, bent or corroded frames, jammed slider tracks, missing screens that need to be fabricated from scratch, or hardware failures (springs, clips, latches) that prevent the screen from sitting properly in the window. We handle all of them on-site, and we keep the materials on the truck to do most jobs in a single visit.

For Culver City specifically, the most common requests are: Spanish-style period homes with original screens, family-home pet/kid damage, commercial work for the studio-adjacent businesses, accessory dwelling unit (ADU) screen fabrication. We come prepared for these conditions on every visit.

For homes with kids, dogs, or cats, we recommend upgrading to pet-resistant mesh during your next re-mesh — it’s significantly stronger than standard fiberglass and prevents most of the tear damage that brings homeowners back to us repeatedly.

Services we offer

Complete screen repair in Culver City.

Every service done on-site — one visit, one invoice.

Re-meshing

Standard fiberglass, pet-resistant, solar, or blackout mesh installed in your existing frames. Cleanest visual result, lowest cost path.

Re-fitting

Adjusting screens that no longer sit properly in their tracks or openings — usually because the frame has bent or the spline has degraded.

New screen fabrication

Building entirely new screens to fit any window opening, including custom shapes, oversized openings, and architectural windows.

Sliding door screens

Re-meshing, track cleaning, roller replacement, and full slider-screen rebuilds. Common request on Culver City homes with sliding glass walls.

Frame repair & replacement

Bent corners, snapped frames, and corroded sections rebuilt or replaced. We carry common frame stock on the truck.

Hardware repair

Springs, clips, latches, and pulls. The small parts that hold the screen in place are often the actual failure point.
