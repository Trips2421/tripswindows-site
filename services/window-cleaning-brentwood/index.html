Estate and tree environment

Brentwood combines large estates with mature, tree-heavy landscaping.

Brentwood is defined by two features: oak trees and open space. The mature oaks that make Brentwood beautiful also shed leaves, acorns, sap, and pollen constantly. Combined with estate irrigation systems that spray hard water across windows, Brentwood homes accumulate mineral deposits and organic debris faster than most LA neighborhoods.

We recommend spring and fall service for Brentwood: spring catches winter's accumulated debris and early-season pollen, fall prepares before the rainy season and catches late-summer oak debris. Many Brentwood homes benefit from three-times-yearly service during peak spring pollen. Our crews are experienced with large properties, estate-scale window counts, and tree management.

How often should you clean windows in Brentwood?

Oak trees and hard water demand strategic timing.

Standard twice yearly

Spring and fall for most Brentwood homes. Spring catches winter debris and spring pollen, fall prepares before rainy season.

Heavy oak exposure

Three times yearly (spring, summer, fall) if your property is surrounded by large oaks with significant shedding.

Multi-level estates

Quarterly for large multi-story properties with significant window counts. Regular service keeps all windows clear and maintained.

Seasonal pollen alert

Extra spring service if severe allergies or visible pollen buildup. Brentwood pollen season can be intense.
