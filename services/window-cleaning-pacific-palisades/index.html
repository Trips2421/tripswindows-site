Bluff and tree environment

Pacific Palisades faces a unique combination of salt spray and heavy tree coverage.

Homes perched on coastal bluffs get constant ocean wind and salt spray. Meanwhile, the neighborhoods inland are heavily tree-lined, creating different challenges: leaves, tree sap, and spring pollen accumulation on windows and gutters. Many Pacific Palisades properties face both challenges — they're on bluffs surrounded by protective landscaping.

This means your windows need both coastal salt-spray expertise and pollen management. We recommend quarterly service for bluff-top homes and twice yearly for inland properties. Pacific Palisades cleaning schedule

How often should you clean windows in Pacific Palisades?

Frequency varies by bluff and tree exposure.

Bluff-top homes

Quarterly (every 3 months) for maximum coastal protection. Ocean wind and salt spray concentration is highest on bluffs.

Inland tree-lined neighborhoods

Twice yearly (spring and fall) for homes away from the bluff but surrounded by mature trees. Spring catches pollen, fall catches debris.

Mixed exposure properties

Three times yearly if your home is bluff-adjacent with significant tree coverage. Spring, summer, and fall recommended.

Commercial retail

Monthly to biweekly for Palisades Village and retail areas. Professional appearance essential for customer perception.
