Why gutter cleaning is critical in Calabasas.

Calabasas sits at the western edge of the San Fernando Valley, framed by the Santa Monica Mountains and surrounded by mature oak canopy and open chaparral. That setting is what makes the city beautiful — and what makes gutter maintenance an essential part of owning a home here. Coast live oaks are evergreen, so they shed leaves and acorns every month of the year, not just in fall. Sycamores along canyon drainages add bark and seed pods. Open hillsides drop dry brush across nearby properties during every Santa Ana wind event.

All of that material ends up in gutters, in roof valleys, and against the wall-flashing of Calabasas homes. In the rainy season, clogged gutters on hillside lots can overflow and undermine retaining walls, erode pad foundations, and saturate soil that holds the structure. In the dry season — especially fall and early winter, when Santa Anas line up with low humidity — that same accumulated debris becomes ember catch on a home in a high fire severity zone. Trip’s Windows clears gutters, roof valleys, and the underside of solar arrays with both seasons in mind.

We coordinate with HOA management at The Oaks, Park Calabasas, and other gated communities, and we’re comfortable with steep driveways, narrow service roads, and the multi-roof complexity that Calabasas estates often involve.
