South Bay coastal protection

Manhattan Beach sits directly on the Pacific — windows face constant ocean exposure.

Manhattan Beach has some of the harshest salt-spray conditions on the LA coast. Homes on the pier and beachfront face constant marine haze and salt residue. Even homes a few blocks inland still deal with significant salt spray. The white, crusty mineral deposits that accumulate on glass aren't just dirt — they're salt that corrodes windows and frames if left untreated.

We recommend quarterly service for all Manhattan Beach homes within a mile of the beach, and bimonthly (every 6 weeks) for beachfront properties. The ocean wind delivers salt spray constantly, so regular maintenance prevents expensive window replacement down the line. Our South Bay crews understand Manhattan Beach's unique coastal environment and provide specialized service.
