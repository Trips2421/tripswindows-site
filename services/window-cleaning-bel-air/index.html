Bel Air cleaning schedule

How often should you clean windows in Bel Air?

Frequency depends on tree coverage, irrigation, and nearby construction activity.

Standard hillside estates

Quarterly service recommended. Bel Air's heavy tree canopy, active construction environment, and irrigation systems mean glass gets dirty faster than typical Los Angeles properties.

Properties with less exposure

Twice yearly (spring and fall) for homes with less tree coverage and fewer nearby construction projects. Still recommended before entertaining season and holidays.

Post-construction or renovation

After any construction work — yours or your neighbor's — a deep clean removes fine dust films, plaster residue, and paint overspray that settle on glass during renovation.

Pre-event or pre-listing

Before major entertaining, open houses, or listing a property. Clean glass is essential for showcasing Bel Air's panoramic views to their full potential.
