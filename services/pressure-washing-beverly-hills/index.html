Why Beverly Hills properties need specialized pressure washing.

Beverly Hills homes are architectural investments. Motor courts with marble or tumbled stone, resort-style pool decks with travertine pavers, and manicured driveways are centerpieces of property appeal and resale value. But hard water (endemic to the foothills area), seasonal pollen, and dust accumulation can dull these surfaces within months.

Beverly Hills' elevated terrain and tree-lined streets mean heavy eucalyptus and oak pollen settling on paved surfaces. Combined with hard water minerals from irrigation, concrete and pavers develop stubborn white stains that cannot be cleaned with a hose. Pool surrounds become slippery from algae in the shade of mature trees. The Trousdale Estates and other hillside areas experience heavier dust and pollen than lower neighborhoods.

Luxury property owners typically schedule pressure washing 2–3 times yearly — spring pollen removal, mid-summer for entertaining season, and fall preparation. Many pair it with window cleaning for complete property refresh before showing or events.
