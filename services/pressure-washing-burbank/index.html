Why Burbank properties need regular pressure washing.

The San Fernando Valley experiences intense summer heat and heavy dust accumulation year-round. Burbank, positioned in the heart of the Valley, faces relentless dust settling from Burbank Boulevard traffic corridors and seasonal pollen from the surrounding foothills. Unlike coastal areas where salt spray is the primary concern, Burbank properties accumulate thick dust layers that household cleaning cannot penetrate.

Burbank's abundant tree cover (particularly in residential neighborhoods near Magnolia Park and the Olive Avenue area) creates shade that accelerates algae and mildew growth on pool decks and patios. Hard water minerals from the local water supply stain concrete and stone. The combination of Valley dust, pollen, hard water deposits, and shade-driven biological growth makes regular pressure washing essential.

Most Burbank homeowners schedule 1–2 pressure washings yearly — spring for heavy dust and pollen removal, and late summer before entertaining or pool season. Many pair it with window cleaning for complete seasonal refresh.
