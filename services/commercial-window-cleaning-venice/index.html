What's included

Complete commercial window cleaning for Venice storefronts and offices.

Every service includes interior and exterior glass, frame care, and salt-spray treatment for coastal environments.

Storefront glass

Exterior storefronts cleaned to perfection using purified water and squeegee techniques. We remove salt spray, mineral deposits, and environmental buildup that damage your curb appeal and invite customers in.

Interior & exterior

Both sides of every window cleaned and polished. For retail spaces, we're careful around merchandise and displays. For restaurants, we protect seating areas while we work.

Specialized treatment for hard water stains and mineral deposits. We use techniques designed to neutralize salt residue that standard methods miss.
