Why gutter cleaning matters in Venice.

Venice sits at sea level on flat terrain — which means water doesn't naturally flow away from your property the way it does on hillsides. Every inch of elevation matters. When gutters clog with leaves from Venice's ficus, fig, and pepper trees, marine layer salt debris, and shingle grit, water has nowhere to go. It backs up, overflows, and runs down walls and onto patios instead of through downspouts.

In most LA neighborhoods, a clogged gutter is an inconvenience. In Venice, it's a water management problem. Homes near the Venice Canals and low-lying zones west of Pacific Avenue are especially vulnerable to pooling water around foundations. When the rare big rain comes — November through March — you need to know your gutters are clear and functioning.

Trip's crew cleans every gutter, flushes every downspout to ground level, and sweeps roof edges so debris doesn't wash back in. We know Venice's drainage challenges firsthand — it's where we're based.
