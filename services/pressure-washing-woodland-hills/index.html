West Valley surface specialists

Woodland Hills driveways and patios accumulate grime faster than coastal properties.

The western San Fernando Valley delivers relentless heat that accelerates surface deterioration. Concrete driveways absorb dust and oil stains that darken over months of Valley summer. Stucco walls — the dominant exterior finish on Woodland Hills homes from the 1950s through new construction — collect airborne particulate that dulls their finish. Pool decks in shaded areas develop algae growth that becomes slippery and unsightly, while sun-exposed decks fade under mineral deposits from splashing.

We use variable-pressure techniques matched to each surface. High-pressure cleaning restores concrete driveways and walkways. Soft-wash methods protect delicate stucco and painted surfaces. Woodland Hills washing schedule

How often should you pressure wash in Woodland Hills?

Valley conditions accelerate surface grime — here's the right cadence.

Standard annual service

Once yearly in spring or fall for most driveways and patios. Removes a full year of Valley heat and dust accumulation from hardscapes.

Pool homes twice yearly

Spring and fall for properties with pool decks. Spring removes winter algae, fall clears summer mineral deposits and prepares for entertaining season.

Stucco exteriors annually

Once yearly for stucco walls and exterior surfaces. Annual soft washing prevents buildup from becoming permanent discoloration on the finish.

Post-fire season

After significant Santa Ana events or nearby fire activity, ash and soot settle on Woodland Hills surfaces. Post-event cleaning prevents staining and protects surface integrity.
