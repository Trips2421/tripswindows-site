Santa Monica cleaning schedule

How often should you clean windows in Santa Monica?

Coastal location and retail corridor demand different frequencies.

Ocean Park beachfront homes

Quarterly (every 3 months) for maximum salt spray protection. These properties face the harshest coastal conditions.

Residential inland Santa Monica

Twice yearly (spring and fall) for homes away from the immediate coast. Still coastal, but less salt spray concentration than Ocean Park.

Montana Avenue retail storefronts

Weekly or biweekly depending on foot traffic and location. Clean windows directly impact retail performance and brand perception.

Downtown Santa Monica commercial

Monthly to biweekly for office and service businesses downtown. Professional appearance matters for client relationships.
