Valley dust and pool expertise

Sherman Oaks homes face Valley heat and pool-related window challenges.

Sherman Oaks sits in the San Fernando Valley with intense afternoon heat. Many homes feature swimming pools, and chlorine-treated pool water creates additional window challenges. When pools run, chlorine spray and mineral-heavy water droplets coat windows and frames. Combined with Valley dust that bakes onto glass in 100-degree heat, Sherman Oaks windows accumulate deposits faster than most LA neighborhoods.

We recommend spring and fall service for most Sherman Oaks homes, with summer service recommended for properties with active pools. Spring cleaning removes winter dust and prepares for heavy pool season. Fall service removes summer accumulation and prepares before rainy season.
